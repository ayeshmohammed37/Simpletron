07        +4220        BRANCHZERO to 20      "If N == 0, jump to Print Phase"

11        +4117        BRANCHNEG to 17       "If Temp > Largest, jump to Update Phase"

17        +2025        LOAD 25               [UPDATE] Load Temp
18        +2124        STORE 24              Store Temp as new Largest
19        +4012        BRANCH to 12          Jump back to Resume Phase
20        +1124        WRITE 24              [PRINT] Output the Largest number
21        +4300        HALT                  Terminate normal execution
22        +4300        HALT                  Terminate early execution
